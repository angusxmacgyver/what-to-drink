# What To Drink

A personal whisky cellar: the bottles still on hand, and the record of what was taken from them.

## Identity

**Bottle**:
One physical bottle. Several bottles can be the same expression.
_Avoid_: Stock, unit

**Backup**:
A closed bottle of an expression, shown collapsed as closed and where it sits.
_Avoid_: Spare, extra, stock

**Expression**:
A particular whisky — the producer and the bottling — shared by every bottle of it.
_Avoid_: Product, SKU, bottling

**House**:
The place a bottle is kept when it is not at the apartment.
_Avoid_: Home

**Apartment**:
The other place a bottle can be kept.
_Avoid_: Flat

## Casks

**Primary cask**:
The kind of cask or casks a bottle's whisky matured in for most of its time, written as short tags such as Ex-bourbon or Oloroso sherry. A bottle with none recorded has no primary cask.
_Avoid_: Cask type, SMWS cask, main cask

**Secondary cask**:
The kind of cask or casks the whisky was moved into to finish, written as short tags such as Rum or PX sherry. A bottle with none recorded has no secondary cask.
_Avoid_: Finish, secondary maturation

**SMWS cask**:
The Society's own description of the cask a Society bottle came from. It is shown with the bottle and is not a primary cask.
_Avoid_: Primary cask, cask type

**Secondary maturation**:
The Society's own account of how a Society bottle moved between casks. It is shown with the bottle and is not a secondary cask.
_Avoid_: Secondary cask, finish

## Choosing bottles

**Library filters**:
Choices that narrow which bottles the Library shows. A Pick is chosen from the bottles they leave showing.
_Avoid_: Pick criteria, facets

**Match exactly**:
A way of applying Library filters in which every selected choice must be true of a bottle, even two choices within one category. With it off, choices within one category are alternatives.
_Avoid_: Match all, any/all, strict

**Pick modifier**:
A choice that changes how a Pick is chosen, never which bottles it can be chosen from. Anything that narrows the bottles is a Library filter instead.
_Avoid_: Pick criteria, Pick option, filter

**I feel fancy**:
A Pick modifier that shows the Pick landing on the fancy wheel. It never changes which bottle is chosen, and it clears after each Pick.
_Avoid_: Fancy mode

**Favor last third**:
A Pick modifier that makes each last-third bottle three times as likely to be the Pick as any other bottle showing. It stays on until it is turned off or the app is reloaded.
_Avoid_: Weighted, Last third only

## Taking a dram

**Pick a dram**:
Asking for a random suggestion of what to pour, drawn from the bottles the Library is showing, to cut an absurd number of options down to one. It never records a dram.
_Avoid_: Pour, roll, Pick a Dram screen

**Pick**:
One bottle chosen at random, from the bottles the Library is showing, to walk to. It is chosen at the House unless the Apartment is chosen. Choosing it does not record a taking.
_Avoid_: Pour, dram, Pick criteria

**Dram**:
A recorded taking from one bottle. It stays with that bottle, in the graveyard too, and it is not removed one at a time.
_Avoid_: Pour, pick, glass

**Dram count**:
How many drams have been recorded from one live bottle. It includes drams that have fallen off Recently poured.
_Avoid_: Volume, level, fill, remaining

**Pour This Dram**:
The owner action that records a dram from one bottle, once the owner PIN has been entered. A dram is never recorded any other way.
_Avoid_: Pouring now, Recently poured, pick, pour one

**Recently poured**:
The 25 most recent drams, kept with the shared cellar and shown in Analytics. A line remains here after its bottle is killed, until it ages out.
_Avoid_: Just poured, recent drams, history, pour log

**Last Poured**:
The mark on the expression whose bottle gave the newest dram. If that dram was a kill shot, the mark is on that bottle in the graveyard until the next dram.
_Avoid_: Last Taste, last pour, latest, most recent

**Kill shot**:
The dram that emptied its bottle. After the bottle is killed, the bottle keeps the fact that a kill shot finished it.
_Avoid_: Kill, last dram, finish

**Last third**:
A bottle whose remaining whisky is in its last third.
_Avoid_: Low, nearly empty, running out

**Graveyard**:
A killed bottle, kept as its details, its drams, the day it was finished, and whether a kill shot finished it. It is no longer in the Library and cannot be picked.
_Avoid_: Empty, fallen

**Date finished**:
The day a bottle was sent to the graveyard.
_Avoid_: Date emptied

**Revive**:
Returning a graveyard bottle to the Library as an open bottle. A kill shot that finished it becomes an ordinary dram.
_Avoid_: Undo kill, restore
