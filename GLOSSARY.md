# What To Drink

A personal whisky cellar: the bottles still on hand, and the record of what was taken from them.

## Identity

**Bottle**:
One physical bottle. Several bottles can be the same expression.
_Avoid_: Stock, unit

**Backup**:
A closed bottle of an expression, shown collapsed as closed and where it sits.
_Avoid_: Spare, extra, stock

**Expression**:
A particular whisky — the producer and the bottling — shared by every bottle of it.
_Avoid_: Product, SKU

**House**:
The place a bottle is kept when it is not at the apartment.
_Avoid_: Home

**Apartment**:
The other place a bottle can be kept.
_Avoid_: Flat

## Casks

**Primary cask**:
The kind of cask or casks a bottle's whisky matured in for most of its time, written as short tags such as Ex-bourbon or Oloroso sherry. A bottle with none recorded has no primary cask.
_Avoid_: Cask type, SMWS cask, main cask

**Secondary cask**:
The kind of cask or casks the whisky was moved into to finish, written as short tags such as Rum or PX sherry. A bottle with none recorded has no secondary cask.
_Avoid_: Finish, secondary maturation

**SMWS cask**:
The Society's own description of the cask a Society bottle came from. It is shown with the bottle and is not a primary cask.
_Avoid_: Primary cask, cask type

**Secondary maturation**:
The Society's own account of how a Society bottle moved between casks. It is shown with the bottle and is not a secondary cask.
_Avoid_: Secondary cask, finish

## Choosing bottles

**Library filters**:
Choices that narrow which bottles the Library shows. A Pick is chosen from the bottles they leave showing.
_Avoid_: Pick criteria, facets

**Match exactly**:
A way of applying Library filters in which every selected choice must be true of a bottle, even two choices within one category. With it off, choices within one category are alternatives.
_Avoid_: Match all, any/all, strict

## Taking a dram

**Pick**:
One bottle chosen at random, from the bottles the Library is showing, to walk to. It is chosen at the House unless the Apartment is chosen. Choosing it does not record a taking.
_Avoid_: Pour, dram, Pick criteria

**Dram**:
A recorded taking from one live bottle. It stays with that bottle until the bottle is killed, and it is not removed one at a time.
_Avoid_: Pour, pick, glass

**Dram count**:
How many drams have been recorded from one live bottle. It includes drams that have fallen off Just poured.
_Avoid_: Volume, level, fill, remaining

**Pouring now**:
The owner action that records a dram from one bottle.
_Avoid_: Just poured, pick, pour one

**Just poured**:
The 25 most recent drams, kept with the shared cellar. A line remains here after its bottle is killed, until it ages out.
_Avoid_: Recent drams, history, pour log

**Kill shot**:
The dram that emptied its bottle. After the bottle is killed, the bottle keeps the fact that a kill shot finished it.
_Avoid_: Kill, last dram, finish

**Last third**:
A bottle whose remaining whisky is in its last third.
_Avoid_: Low, nearly empty, running out

**Graveyard**:
A killed bottle, kept as its details, the day it was finished, and whether a kill shot finished it. It is no longer in the Library and cannot be picked.
_Avoid_: Empty, fallen

**Date finished**:
The day a bottle was sent to the graveyard.
_Avoid_: Date emptied
